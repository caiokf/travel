## Zagreb

The starting point of this journey. Croatia's capital with its mix of Austro-Hungarian architecture and vibrant cafe culture.

## Vukovar

Five days of cycling through the Croatian countryside, following the Sava River eastward toward the Danube.

## Novi Sad

Crossing into Serbia, arriving at the "Serbian Athens" - a city of culture, music, and the famous Petrovaradin Fortress.

## Belgrade

The confluence of the Sava and Danube rivers. A city with layers of history and an energy unlike anywhere else in the Balkans.

## Timisoara

Into Romania, reaching the city that sparked the 1989 revolution. Beautiful squares and a welcoming atmosphere after long days on the road.

## Cluj Napoca

The final destination. The heart of Transylvania, surrounded by hills and filled with university life. A fitting end to 28 days in the saddle.
