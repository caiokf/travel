## Tehran

The journey began in Iran's sprawling capital, where modernity and tradition collide in fascinating ways.

## Isfahan

"Half the world" as the Persian saying goes. The stunning Islamic architecture and the bridges over the Zayandeh River left an indelible impression.

## Ghoortan Citadel

An ancient mud-brick fortress rising from the desert, a testament to Persian ingenuity and resilience.

## Varzaneh Desert

Golden sand dunes stretching to the horizon, with the silence of the desert offering a profound sense of peace.

## Kerman

Gateway to the southeastern deserts, where the famous Ganjali Khan Complex showcases the height of Safavid architecture.

## Yazd

The ancient city of wind towers and Zoroastrian heritage, where narrow alleyways lead to unexpected courtyards and warm welcomes.

## Shiraz

City of poets, gardens, and wine. The nearby ruins of Persepolis brought the grandeur of ancient Persia to life.
