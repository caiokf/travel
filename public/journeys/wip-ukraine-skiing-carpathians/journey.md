## Kiev

The journey began in Ukraine's capital, where snow blanketed the golden domes of ancient churches and the city hummed with winter energy. Kiev served as the perfect launching point before heading west toward the mountains.

## Lviv

A few days later, the train pulled into Lviv - a city of cobblestone streets, aromatic coffee houses, and Austro-Hungarian architecture dusted with frost. The cultural heart of western Ukraine offered a taste of Old Europe before the mountain adventure ahead.

## Bukovel

The crown jewel of the trip awaited in the Carpathians. Bukovel, Ukraine's premier ski resort, delivered fresh powder, well-groomed runs, and that unmistakable mountain atmosphere. Days were spent carving through snow-covered slopes, nights warming up with hearty Ukrainian cuisine and good company.
