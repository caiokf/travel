## Colombo

Arrived in the bustling capital city, where colonial architecture meets modern Sri Lankan life.

## Midigama

A laid-back surf village on the southern coast, known for its consistent waves and relaxed atmosphere.

## Hikkaduwa

Beach town famous for its coral reefs, sea turtles, and vibrant nightlife.

## Galle

The jewel of the south - a UNESCO World Heritage Dutch fort town with cobblestone streets and ocean views.

## Mirissa

Palm-fringed beach paradise and the best place in Sri Lanka for whale watching.

## Ella

A mountain village wrapped in tea plantations, waterfalls, and the famous Nine Arches Bridge.

## Nuwara Eliya

"Little England" in the highlands - tea estates, cool climate, and misty mountain scenery.
