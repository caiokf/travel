Eight countries. Three thousand kilometers. Fifty-five days of thumb and patience and the kindness of strangers who became, however briefly, companions.

The Balkans taught something the Alps never could: that borders are lines on maps, but roads run through them all the same. That language barriers dissolve when you share bread. That the further east you go, the more insistent the hospitality becomes.

The journey east continues.
